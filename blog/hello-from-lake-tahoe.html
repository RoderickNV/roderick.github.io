Hello from Lake Tahoe

October 7, 2026 · Lake Tahoe, Nevada

I moved to Lake Tahoe to learn to fly, and ended up with a lot more than a pilot's license: 60-plus days a year on the mountain, a Siberian husky named Lobo who hikes with me every week, and a side project that turned into Cash Balancer.

This blog is where I'll write about all of it. Flights over the Sierra, days on the snow, trails around the lake, and what I'm learning building credit and lending products and a free app that helps young adults get out of debt.

What to expect

Short posts, lots of photos, and the occasional longer piece on product work. If something here is useful to you, the best way to reach me is a message on LinkedIn.
